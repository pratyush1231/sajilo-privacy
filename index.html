Privacy Policy and Data Deletion Instructions · Effective 12 October 2026
Privacy PolicyData Deletion

Sajilo is an early, non-commercial proof of concept that helps a business owner post listings (rentals, products and services) on their own Facebook Page and reply quickly to comments on them. It is operated by an individual, Pratyush Pradhanang, and is used only by the owner of the connected Page and invited family members.

Privacy Policy

1. What Sajilo does

When a Page owner connects their Facebook Page to Sajilo, Sajilo reads posts on that Page and the comments people leave on them, so the owner can see every inquiry about a rental listing in one place and mark it as handled. If the owner chooses, Sajilo can publish a listing post or a reply on the owner's Page.

2. Information we collect

  • From the Page owner: name and Facebook user ID as provided by Facebook Login, the connected Page's name and ID, and an access token that lets Sajilo act on that Page.
  • From the connected Page: post IDs, post text, post links and times, and counts of reactions, shares and comments.
  • From comments on the Page's posts: comment ID, comment text, time, and, only if Facebook provides it, the commenter's public name and ID.
  • Listing details the owner types into Sajilo, such as rent, deposit, location and photos.

Sajilo does not collect passwords, payment information, private messages, friend lists or any data from Facebook profiles other than what is listed above.

3. How we use it

  • To show the owner new comments on their listings and how long each has been waiting.
  • To link each comment to the right rental listing.
  • To publish posts or replies on the owner's Page when the owner asks.
  • To keep the connection working (for example, warning the owner when Facebook access needs renewing).

We do not sell, rent or share this information with anyone, do not use it for advertising, and do not use it to build profiles of commenters.

4. Storage and security

Data is stored on secured servers used only for this project. Access tokens are encrypted at rest and never shown in the app. Only the operator and the connected Page owner can access the data.

5. Retention

Data is kept only while the Page is connected and the proof of concept is running. When the owner disconnects the Page, or when the project ends, the related data is deleted within 30 days.

6. Your choices

  • Page owners can disconnect Sajilo at any time in Facebook: Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations (or Apps and websites) → remove “Sajilo”.
  • People who commented on a connected Page can ask for their comments to be removed from Sajilo, as described below. Deleting a comment on Facebook also removes it from Sajilo at the next sync.

7. Children

Sajilo is not intended for anyone under 18 and does not knowingly collect their data.

8. Changes

Data Deletion Instructions

You can have your data removed from Sajilo at any time.

If you connected your Facebook Page

  1. Open Facebook and go to Settings & privacy → Settings.
  2. Open Business integrations (or Apps and websites).
  3. Find Sajilo and choose Remove.
  4. Optionally email pratyush.pradhanang7@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my Sajilo data” and your Page name.

Once Sajilo is removed or we receive your email, all data for your Page — access token, posts, comments and listings — is deleted within 30 days, and we reply to confirm.

If you commented on a connected Page

Email pratyush.pradhanang7@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my Sajilo data”, the Page name, and the text or approximate time of your comment. We will delete the matching comment records within 30 days and confirm by email. Deleting your comment on Facebook also removes it from Sajilo automatically.
